# anti-ai-tells

## When this workflow applies

Use to rewrite or audit non-academic prose that is generic, formulaic, repetitive, or overly polished while preserving facts, voice, scope, and high-stakes qualifiers. Trigger for professional messages, reports, resumes, tickets, explanations, and complete drafts. Route scholarly manuscripts to academic-manuscript-copyedit. Do not evade disclosure, disguise plagiarism, fabricate content, or promise detector outcomes.

## Web execution boundary

This Web edition provides analysis, planning, review, templates, and verification guidance. It does not imply that a scanner, cloud account, repository, endpoint, HSM, SIEM, SOAR, or other external system is connected. Begin read-only. Require explicit authorization, confirmed scope, available tools, and rollback conditions before any live action.

## Route first

Use this skill for non-academic finished writing or audits.
Route instead to academic-manuscript-copyedit when the primary artifact is a manuscript, dissertation-derived paper, journal article, book chapter, scholarly reference list, reviewer response, or venue submission.
Do not trigger for a factual answer, code generation, translation of a short phrase, brainstorming, or a request that does not require a finished rewritten artifact.

## Rewrite process

1. Identify the concrete purpose, audience, requested action, and facts.
2. Remove filler, generic praise, theatrical contrast, and unsupported intensifiers.
3. Replace vague abstractions with the supplied specifics.
4. Vary sentence structure only where it improves readability.
5. Keep legitimate technical terminology.
6. Preserve the writer's voice instead of applying a universal “human” persona.
7. Read the result for accidental overclaiming, sarcasm, rudeness, or ambiguity.
8. Deliver the complete usable text in the requested format.

## Integrity boundaries

Refuse or redirect requests whose purpose is to:
- Defeat a detector while concealing AI assistance
- Falsely claim sole authorship
- Manufacture originality, experience, sources, citations, credentials, or results
- Remove a required disclosure
- Disguise plagiarism
A safe redirection is to improve clarity, specificity, voice, and accurate disclosure.

## Output check

Before sending, confirm:
- Meaning and material facts are unchanged.
- Required detail was not shortened away.
- The result sounds natural for the actual audience and domain.
- No formulaic pattern was replaced with a different formulaic pattern.
- No unsupported fact, emotion, relationship, or commitment was introduced.

## Required output discipline

- Separate observed evidence, source-supported facts, inference, assumptions, and recommendations.
- State tool and data limitations explicitly.
- Preserve exact technical literals, measurements, citations, and user-approved constraints.
- Provide validation steps, unresolved issues, residual risk, and the next authorization gate.
- Never claim execution, access, containment, compliance, certification, or verification that was not observed.
